The Label

A label for the way you actually live.

MANIESTA LABEL is a premium contemporary fashion house based in Pakistan. We make a small, deliberate catalogue of pieces we want to wear ourselves - cut from fabrics that hold up, finished quietly, and priced without theatre.

Editorial - 01

Cotton from Faisalabad. Cut in Karachi.

Pakistan grows some of the finest long-staple cotton in the world. We buy it directly from spinning mills in Faisalabad and cut each piece by hand in Karachi - small runs, considered construction, no shortcuts.

Our story

Built around a small, deliberate catalogue.

We started MANIESTA because we were tired of buying the same things twice. Clothes that felt good on day one and fell apart by month three. Pieces that promised a cut and delivered a compromise. We wanted the opposite - a short list of things worth owning, made properly the first time.

Every silhouette in the catalogue earns its place. We cut, wear, and rewrite each piece dozens of times before it ships. If something isn't right, it doesn't go out. That's why there aren't thirty items for sale at any given time. There are seven or eight. And every one of them is meant to be worn for years.

Principles

01 - Fabric

Long-staple or nothing.

We buy from mills we've visited ourselves. No middlemen, no mystery blends - combed cottons, brushed twills, garment-dyed jerseys, loopback terry.

02 - Make

Small runs, considered construction.

Numbered batches. Single-needle seams where it matters. No shortcuts, no fast-fashion timelines, no compromising on the finish.

03 - Wear

Designed to be worn for years.

We don't chase seasons. We refine. Every piece is cut to layer with what you already own and disappear into the way you actually dress.

A note from the founder

"I started MANIESTA because I couldn't find what I wanted to wear. Nothing fit right, nothing lasted, and everything looked like it was designed for someone else. So we built the label we wanted - small, slow, and honest about what it costs to make something well."

Usman Murtaza
Founder, MANIESTA LABEL
